THE CHALLENGE

Reactive navigation cannot afford to misunderstand the scene.

Conventional mapless policies flatten local sensor readings and often miss the relations between nearby obstacles. They can also require extensive exploration before learning safe behaviour. GAVEL-TD3 addresses both limits with structured perception and a training-only motion prior.

METHOD

Two complementary ideas,
one end-to-end policy.

01

Graph-attention state representation

Each LiDAR sector becomes a node. A directed graph and two-layer GAT learn which local obstacle relations matter, while the PCR readout preserves the robot and goal state.

R
02

APF-guided value learning

An artificial potential field provides reference velocities during early training. An auxiliary critic turns this prior into faster value learning, then naturally yields to learned exploration.

GR
LOCAL LIDAR→BUILD GRAPH→GAT + PCR→TD3 POLICY→VELOCITY COMMANDS
